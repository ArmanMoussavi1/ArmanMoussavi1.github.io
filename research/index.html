Research Highlight

TANGO

Topology-Aware Neural Graph Operator for material constitutive laws
ICLR 2026 • AI4MATSCI Workshop ICLR Travel Grant

TANGO is a graph neural operator that predicts the full nonlinear stress–strain response of polymer networks directly from their molecular topology, fast enough to make inverse design of network architecture possible.

Moussavi, A., Yildirim, A. B., & Keten, S. “Topology-Aware Neural Graph Operator (TANGO) for Material Constitutive Laws.” ICLR 2026 AI4MATSCI Workshop.
